03 / UNDERSTAND

A review aid, not a security verdict

A non-human identity represents software or a workload. Its credential proves identity or conveys authority; its permissions determine reachable operations. An AI agent can use several such identities as it selects and chains tools.

This planner uses 12 transparent, practitioner-authored control questions. “No” produces a warning; “Not known” requests evidence; “Yes” records a self-reported control. Read-only paths skip the high-impact write-approval check. Paths without delegation skip the delegation check. Unknown context never skips either. Administrative authority always adds a warning.

Counts indicate review coverage, not risk, probability, or compliance. The planner does not inspect IAM policies, credentials, cloud accounts, model behavior, or actual enforcement. Read-only access can still leak data. Review each materially different path separately.

Answers stay in page memory. Reset clears the form and report; it cannot remove downloaded files or clipboard copies. Export only when you intend to share or retain the report. Browser extensions and browser-managed history or form restoration are outside this application's control.
